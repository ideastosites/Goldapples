## 6. FRAMEWORKS PAGE

### Our Communication Frameworks

**Original thinking for practical communication problems.**

Goldapples is building a body of frameworks to help leaders and organisations understand communication beyond information sharing — guiding our advisory work, training, diagnostics and thought leadership.

**Physics of Communication**
Communication behaves like movement — a message has a source, force, direction, medium, resistance, distortion and effect. This framework explains why a message that seems clear at the source can arrive weakened or misunderstood, helping leaders examine the force behind a message, the medium carrying it, the resistance it meets, and its actual effect on the audience.

**The M.E.A.N.I.N.G. Framework**
A Goldapples tool for designing messages that do more than pass information — helping leaders understand how meaning is created, passed, received, interpreted and acted upon. A message can be well-written and still fail; a policy can be announced and still resisted. This framework helps reduce ambiguity, prevent meaning drift and strengthen communication outcomes.

**Leadership Alignment Framework**
Helps leadership teams keep strategy, message, behaviour and execution moving in the same direction — focused on the relationship between leadership intent, message clarity, internal interpretation, behavioural consistency, stakeholder trust and execution outcomes.

**Leadership Communication Lab**
A practical learning environment where leaders test, refine and improve how they communicate decisions, strategy, change and difficult messages — built around scenarios, simulations, peer feedback and communication diagnostics.
